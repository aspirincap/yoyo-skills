# Evaluation Rubric

Score each candidate from 1 to 5. Prefer products with low procurement cost, low MOQ, compact dimensions, clear use case, and low compliance risk.

| Dimension | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Procurement cost | Very low cost with room for 3x+ markup | Acceptable, needs bundling or differentiation | Too high for the target price band |
| MOQ friendliness | Low MOQ or sample-friendly | Moderate MOQ | High MOQ or unclear MOQ |
| Logistics friendliness | Small, light, durable, non-liquid, non-battery | Manageable, needs packaging check | Bulky, fragile, liquid, magnetic, battery, or hazardous |
| Compliance risk | Low-risk general goods | Minor labeling/material concerns | Battery/electrical/children/food-contact/medical/IP risk |
| Differentiation | Easy to bundle, brand, customize, or create content | Generic but usable | Commodity with severe price war |
| Supplier credibility | Long-running shop, clear services, complete info | Some info missing | New/unclear shop or poor detail page |

## Suggested Priority Labels

- `A`: Test samples now; good fit and manageable risk.
- `B`: Promising but needs supplier confirmation or market validation.
- `C`: Only keep as reference; risk, MOQ, or price is weak.
- `Avoid`: Likely compliance/IP/logistics issue for the stated market.
