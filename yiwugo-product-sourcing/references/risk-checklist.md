# Risk Checklist

Flag these before recommending bulk purchase.

## Product Risk

- Battery, charger, USB charging, LED electronics: certification, labeling, and shipping restrictions.
- Food-contact products: FDA/LFGB/material declarations and migration testing.
- Children's products: CPSIA, choking hazard, warning labels, age grading.
- Pest control, mosquito repellent, health claims: EPA/FDA/claim substantiation risk.
- Branded shapes, logos, cartoon characters, or patented designs: IP risk.
- Glass, ceramic, fragile, liquid, powder, magnetic products: breakage or restricted shipping.
- Ultra-cheap plastics: odor, durability, bad reviews, returns.

## Sourcing Risk

- High MOQ relative to testing budget.
- Price range is too wide; request exact ladder pricing and SKU mapping.
- Product detail page lacks dimensions, material, package size, or weight.
- Shop category does not match product category; verify supply stability.
- Seasonal goods: buy early and avoid post-season inventory.

## Questions To Ask Suppliers

- 单品尺寸、包装尺寸、单品重量、外箱规、装箱数。
- 是否含电池/磁铁/液体/粉末/木质包装。
- 是否能贴标、换英文包装、做中性包装。
- 样品费、样品交期、大货 MOQ、大货交期。
- 是否有 CE/FCC/RoHS/UL/FDA/LFGB/CPSIA 等文件；证书是否匹配该 SKU。
- 是否支持一件代发、跨境仓、或指定货代发货。
