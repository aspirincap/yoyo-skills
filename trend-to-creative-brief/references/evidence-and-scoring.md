# Evidence and Scoring

## Source strength

| Type | Weight | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| native | 5 | Dated platform-native post, trend page or first-party observation |
| official | 4 | Official platform documentation, newsroom or creative center |
| industry | 3 | Methodologically clear analytics/research provider |
| media | 2 | Reputable editorial summary without direct native dataset |
| community | 1 | Creator/community discussion or anecdotal examples |
| unknown | 0 | Unclear origin; useful only as a lead |

Multiple independent sources improve confidence, but ten weak reposts do not equal native evidence.

## Freshness

- `fresh`: inside the trend's estimated useful window.
- `aging`: inside 1.5× of the useful window; test quickly and recheck.
- `stale`: beyond 1.5×; use only if the mechanic became evergreen.
- `unknown`: no reliable observation date.

Suggested review windows:

- audio/meme/challenge: 7–14 days;
- creator format or editing pattern: 21–45 days;
- seasonal behavior: until the relevant event cutoff;
- search/commerce pattern: 30–90 days, depending on data cadence;
- evergreen narrative mechanic: review quarterly.

## Fit

Evaluate audience, use case, platform, proof, tone and market. A numerical score is only a sorting aid. Always explain why the product naturally participates in the mechanic.

## Confidence

- high: strong dated evidence, clear market scope and natural brand fit;
- medium: partial native proof or fit requires adaptation;
- low: secondary sources, ambiguous dates, or forced brand connection.
