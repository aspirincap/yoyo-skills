Every scorecard item should retain the source URLs and dates so the decision remains auditable.

`engagement`, `uncertainty` and `raw_provenance` are optional evidence fields. Engagement is observed context, not a performance prediction. Direct public-data research should retain query, operation, billing and market-confidence context in the post-level artifact.
