# Creative Adaptation

## Preserve mechanics, replace expression

A mechanic is a reusable structure: reveal, reaction, comparison, confession, list, POV, transformation, comment reply, montage, carousel tutorial or recurring visual beat.

Replace:

- exact words and jokes;
- another creator's persona or likeness;
- copyrighted footage, music, characters and artwork;
- brand-specific visual identity;
- unsupported outcomes or social proof.

## Product truth first

Connect the trend to a visible product fact, demonstration, authentic use case or verifiable customer problem. If the product can be removed and the concept still reads identically, the adaptation is probably superficial.

## Platform translation

- TikTok/Reels/Shorts: hook quickly, show the mechanic early, keep proof visually legible.
- Carousel: turn the same mechanic into a saved/reference sequence rather than forcing motion.
- Paid ad: add product clarity, proof, claim review and CTA without destroying native tone.
- Organic: allow personality and conversation; avoid turning every trend into a direct-response pitch.

## Test design

Change one main hypothesis per variant. Record the learning question rather than claiming predicted uplift.
