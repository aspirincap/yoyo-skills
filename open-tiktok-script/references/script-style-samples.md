# TikTok原创合成脚本样本库

> 这些内容是为本 Skill 编写的合成示例，不是 TikTok 创作者逐字稿，也不代表真实投放表现。仅学习口语节奏、镜头感和 creator-native 语气，不要逐字复制。

## 样本1：美妆底妆（TikTok Shop）

**品类**：美妆/底妆  
**时长**：约28秒  
**钩子类型**：POV + 场景痛点  
**风格标注**：镜前口播、半边脸演示、自然CTA

```text
POV: your makeup looks fine in your bathroom,
and then daylight exposes everything.

I'm trying this on the side that always separates first.
One pump, fingers first,
then I press it in with a sponge.

This is what it looks like next to my usual base.
It's not giving mask.
It's giving actual skin,
but a little more put together.

If you want the shade list,
tap the product card.
I put the one I'm wearing there.
```

**风格拆解**：
- 第一秒有强场景：bathroom vs daylight。
- 卖点来自镜头演示，不靠“完美遮瑕”承诺。
- CTA自然，像创作者在回答评论。
- 安全点：不羞辱肤色、年龄、痘痘或皱纹。

## 样本2：家居收纳（Organic / TikTok Shop）

**品类**：家居/收纳  
**时长**：约24秒  
**钩子类型**：messy before + satisfying demo  
**风格标注**：俯拍、手部动作、ASMR感

```text
This drawer has been stressing me out for months.

Watch how fast this fixes it.
I snapped these dividers in,
sorted the random cables,
and now I can actually see what I own.

The best part is that it doesn't need tools.
I moved it twice before I got the spacing right.

If your desk drawer looks like mine did,
this is in the product card.
```

**风格拆解**：
- 痛点是具体场景，不是攻击用户。
- “watch how fast”制造观看理由。
- 演示可信：提到“moved it twice”增加真实感。
- CTA直接但不硬。

## 样本3：营养补充/日常习惯（Organic）

**品类**：营养补充/健康习惯  
**时长**：约30秒  
**钩子类型**：routine + 个人体验  
**风格标注**：保守功效、生活化表达、低风险

```text
This is the tiny thing I added to my morning routine
because I kept skipping breakfast.

I keep a tube in my work bag,
drop one in cold water,
and it actually tastes like something I'd finish.

I'm not saying it magically changes your life.
For me, it just makes the morning feel less chaotic.

The flavor is light,
the bubbles are fun,
and it takes about ten seconds.

I linked the pack I bought
if you want to try it.
```

**风格拆解**：
- 主动避开夸张健康承诺。
- 用体验和便利性卖点替代“改善身体状态”的硬承诺。
- 适合健康、食品、保健类更敏感品类。

## 样本4：清洁工具（Paid In-Feed / Spark Ads）

**品类**：清洁/工具  
**时长**：约18秒  
**钩子类型**：demo-first  
**风格标注**：快节奏、单一卖点、适合投放

```text
I thought this was going to be another cleaning gimmick.

But look at this corner.
One spray,
one pass,
and the buildup is actually lifting.

I like the angled brush
because it gets into the part I usually avoid.

If you clean around faucets,
this is worth trying.
Tap to shop.
```

**风格拆解**：
- 开头承认怀疑，降低广告感。
- 单一主卖点：angled brush reaches difficult corners。
- “worth trying”比“best ever”更可信。
- 不做无法证明的杀菌/消毒承诺。

## 样本5：穿搭单品（Creator Brief风格）

**品类**：时尚/穿搭  
**时长**：约25秒  
**钩子类型**：fit problem + try-on montage  
**风格标注**：多场景试穿、尺码建议、避免身材羞辱

```text
I needed one top that works with jeans,
work pants,
and the skirt I never know how to style.

This is look one:
casual, half tucked.

Look two:
office, with a belt.

Look three:
dinner, sleeves pushed up.

The fabric is the reason it looks more expensive than it is.
If you like a relaxed fit,
I'd size up.

I linked the color I'm wearing.
```

**风格拆解**：
- 卖点是“多场景可穿”，不是“遮肉/显瘦羞辱”。
- 试穿镜头天然提供留存。
- 尺码建议像真实购买体验。

## TikTok口语风格总结

### 1. 先像人，再像广告

- 用 `I tried...`、`I didn't expect...`、`this is the part I care about...`
- 避免 `Introducing our revolutionary product` 这类品牌腔。

### 2. 一条素材只说一个主线

- 15秒：1个痛点 + 1个演示 + 1个CTA。
- 30秒：最多3个卖点，每个卖点都要有镜头。

### 3. 把抽象卖点翻译成镜头

- `easy to use` → `no tools, I moved it twice, still took under a minute`
- `comfortable` → `I wore it sitting, walking, and reaching overhead`
- `hydrating` → `my skin looked less tight under makeup`

### 4. 允许强表达，但不要虚假承诺

- 可以说 `I'm obsessed`、`weirdly good`、`worth trying`。
- 少说 `guaranteed`、`works for everyone`、`permanent`、`cures`。

### 5. 不用身份羞辱做钩子

- 不写 `girls with bad skin need this`。
- 改成 `if your base separates around your nose, try this step`。
- 不写 `hide your belly`。
- 改成 `a smoother fit under fitted tops`。

### 6. CTA要像平台内行为

- Organic：`save this`、`comment "link"`、`follow for part two`。
- TikTok Shop：`tap the product card`、`check the yellow basket`。
- Paid：`tap to shop`、`try it today`、`see the demo`。
