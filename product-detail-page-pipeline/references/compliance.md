# Claims and Asset Safety

## Evidence levels

- `visible`: directly shown in an authorized source image.
- `supplied`: stated by the user or product documentation.
- `verified`: supported by a provided test, certificate or official specification.
- `inferred`: a planning hypothesis that must not be rendered as fact.
- `unsupported`: exclude from customer-facing copy.

## High-risk claims

Require strong supplied evidence and platform review for health, medical, cosmetic efficacy, safety, environmental, financial, performance, compatibility, certification, warranty and before/after claims.

Do not write guarantees such as “cures,” “permanent,” “zero risk,” “best,” “100%,” or “clinically proven” without suitable evidence and jurisdiction-specific review.

## Comparisons

- Compare observable design or supplied specification, not an unnamed competitor's quality.
- Avoid competitor logos, packaging and near-identical layouts.
- State the comparison basis and avoid manipulating scale or lighting.

## Assets

Use only assets the user owns or may process. Do not publish customer products, competitor captures, generated people, trademarks or licensed fonts without redistribution rights.

## QC

Every customer-facing claim should map to an evidence source in the prompt pack. If the visual does not prove the claim, revise and regenerate that screen rather than relying on text alone or repairing it with an overlay.
